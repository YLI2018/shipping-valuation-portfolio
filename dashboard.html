M1+ · Hedonic price model

Beyond age, what moves a ship's price? M1+ measures how the building yard, a scrubber and special survey timing change the price of ships of the same age and size.

How M1+ compares with M1

Estimated on 297 tanker and bulker sales. Scored on the same 43 MR sales, M1 has the lower average error (7.5% against 8.9%). M1 therefore sets the market value; M1+ explains what drives it.

Full method and technical note: M1+ page in the shipping-vessel-valuation repository on GitHub.

Model roadmap

One case runs through every model: Should we buy this MR tanker? M1 is the base: every later model needs a market value and an age curve.

Next phase: the voyage estimator (VE) and the build-versus-buy model (M4) will be integrated into this case.

Blue box: built and used in this case. Grey dashed box and line: built, but not used in this case (the voyage estimator's values are placeholders in M3). Grey box: planned.

What M1 gives each model

ModelWhat M1 gives it
M2 ValuationToday's market value from comparable sales (one leg of the valuation), and the age curve to value the ship at the end of the cash-flow period
M3 Acquisition and financeThe value that sets the price to pay and the collateral value; the age curve projects that value year by year to test loan-to-value covenants
M4 Build vs buySecond-hand values at each age, to compare with the price of a newbuilding
M1+ Hedonic regressionThe same sales database; M1+ adds size, yard and specification to age and checks whether the curve improves; its results are shown on the Valuation page
M5 DashboardThe M1, M1+ and M2 valuations and the sales database (Overview, Valuation and Database pages); M3 adds the Acquisition and finance page
